## H. New Riyadh opportunities

Riyadh already has the deepest existing coverage of any city (static hub page, the most PSEO service×city combinations, the richest venue content). The 2026-09-13 audit's Day-3 plan is **not** about new Riyadh URLs — it targets two real, already-identified gaps: (1) inconsistent partner-network disclosure on `conferences`/`luxury-vip-events`/`destination-events` (these lean first-party — "our production team," "our full production capability" — while `exhibitions`/`event-production` correctly disclose the partner-network delivery model), and (2) duplicate venue-list copy between `corporate-events` and `conferences` (both list KAFD/RICEC/Ritz-Carlton/Four Seasons/Al Faisaliah/JW Marriott near-verbatim).

**New this pass**: `/locations/alula` — despite AlUla being one of only 4 "primary cities" with full PSEO service-page coverage — has **zero internal links** to its own `/locations/alula/{service}` sub-pages, unlike Riyadh/Jeddah/Dammam which all cross-link correctly. This is a missed internal-linking fix, not a new-page opportunity, but it sits in the same "Riyadh-tier city" bucket the brief asks about.

No new Riyadh-specific city-split pages are recommended — every existing city split (PSEO pages) followed proven national-level demand first, and that discipline should hold for any future page too (mega-events, gala, product-launch, brand-activation should launch national and only split by city once GSC shows real per-city demand).

## I. Hotel-related opportunities

**Full evidence base**: see `phase-0-existing-site-audit.md` §Hotel Opportunity Deep-Dive for the complete reasoning. Summary:

- **Current coverage**: hotels appear only as incidental venue name-drops (trust-bar logos, FAQ mentions) or as a destination for guest transfers/valet — never as their own subject. No page treats "hotel venue sourcing," "hotel event production," or "hotel guest transportation coordination" as a discrete, ownable service.
- **Vendor-capability backing: THIN TO NONE.** The backend `Hospitality` category has zero vendors on file. The `Venues` category has exactly one entry (RCU AlUla heritage villages — not a hotel). The one real, on-record inbound hotel-venue inquiry (Warwick Hotel Khobar, SCEGA-approval request, per `PROJECT-LEDGER.md` 2026-08-19) was triaged and found **no vendor in the network could confidently service it end-to-end** — the transportation partner explicitly declined permit-only involvement, and no vendor was confirmed Khobar-based or SCEGA-licensed.
- **GSC demand signal: ABSENT.** The one "hotel" match in the 3-month GSC export (`AUDIT-03-GSC.md`) is classified under "IRRELEVANT" (AI-assistant-style prompts about Hilton loyalty programs) — no genuine hotel-venue-sourcing or hotel-event-production query appears anywhere in the export.
- **Recommendation-ready verdict**: **Do NOT build a dedicated hotel venue-sourcing/production pillar page yet.** Building one today, given the site's own established pattern of naming real vendor-backed venues in trust bars, would risk the exact "thin/unbacked content" problem this project's hard rules explicitly prohibit. The evidence-supported sequence is: (1) recruit or formally document a real hotel-side relationship (a hotel banquet/events contact, hotel group, or hotel-focused sourcing partner — with particular attention to closing the Eastern-Province/Khobar coverage gap the Warwick inquiry exposed), then (2) revisit the page decision once that capability is real.
- **Lower-risk near-term option, already proven-capability-backed**: expand the existing "Airport & Hotel Transfers" bullet on `/services/vip-transportation` and the "Venue & Hotel Valet" bullet on `/services/valet-parking` with more substance — both are already true today via the Advanced Prestige partnership, so this is an ENRICH of proven capability, not a new unbacked claim. This is a content decision entirely separate from the "should we build a hotel pillar page" question and can proceed independently.
